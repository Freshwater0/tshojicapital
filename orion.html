One address, a complete coastal lifestyle

Designed for life connected to the ocean.

Orion brings private homes, considered hospitality and destination wellness together in one composed Gold Coast address.

Arrival begins with an open ocean axis. Hotel accommodation occupies the lower levels, private residences rise above, and the building culminates in expansive sky homes, the Crown Penthouse and Olympus rooftop hospitality.

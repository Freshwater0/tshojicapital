01

A vertical orchid above the Pacific

Architecture that opens toward the ocean like a living flower.

The Orchid is conceived as a scalloped coastal tower embraced by two sculptural lattice petals. Its public rooms occupy the podium, refined hotel accommodation rises through the middle, and increasingly private residences unfold above.

A compact central core preserves the long curved ocean edge for arrival lounges, primary suites, social rooms and terraces. Pale stone, ivory plaster, pale oak, champagne bronze and restrained ocean tones form one continuous material language.

02

How this gets delivered

No in-house team stands behind this yet — so each stage is run directly, with a named specialist engaged as the project reaches it.

1

Site & feasibility

Candidate land assessed against zoning and overlays; a quantity surveyor engaged to lock real costs before any offer is made.

2

Design & approval

Architectural drawings and floor plans, already complete, go to council with a town planner managing the application through to decision.

3

Capital & construction

Investor equity and construction finance close; a builder is engaged and construction runs in staged drawdowns.

4

Sales & operations

Residences settle to owners; the hotel and wellness floor open and begin operating as the building's ongoing income.

Away from the strip, still on the coast

The target corridor sits between Burleigh Heads, Palm Beach, and Mermaid Beach — deliberately clear of the Gold Coast's busiest tourist stretch, while remaining minutes from the beach itself.

08

Questions an investor would ask

Why is this being raised before land is acquired?+
Investor equity is what makes land acquisition and the resulting development approval bankable in the first place — it's the deposit and buffer a lender and a vendor both need to see before either will commit.
What happens to my capital if the site changes?+
Candidate sites are being assessed against zoning and overlays before any offer is made specifically so the target corridor and scheme are unlikely to change materially after capital is committed. Any change would be disclosed directly.
Who else is involved in delivering this?+
Tshoji Capital is founder-led. Architects, town planners, a quantity surveyor, and legal counsel are engaged as the project reaches each stage — see the process above for where each one comes in.
How were the figures on this page produced?+
Current figures are preliminary estimates based on the completed designs and indicative land pricing. A formal feasibility study, commissioned early in the process above, will confirm or revise them.

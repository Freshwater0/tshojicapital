Designed around the rhythm of the day

Orion is conceived as more than an apartment building: each part of the day has its own place, mood and connection to the coast.

1

Arrive

Enter through a calm, hotel-inspired lobby where the journey through the building begins with light, texture and an ocean axis.

2

Live

Private residences balance generous living, quiet retreats and expansive glazing, with choices shaped around scale and outlook.

3

Restore

Move between strength training, Pilates, poolside restoration and a complete spa experience without leaving the building.

4

Gather

Meet over dinner, settle into the signature bar or rise to Olympus for a rooftop experience above the coastline.

03

Close to the energy, grounded by the coast

Orion is being shaped for the coastal corridor spanning Burleigh Heads, Palm Beach and Mermaid Beach—close to the ocean, dining and everyday essentials while offering a calmer alternative to the busiest tourist precincts.

The final site is under assessment. Location details, views, access and the final development program will be confirmed before sales documentation is released.

08

Questions about Orion

What is Orion?+
Orion is a proposed 21-level mixed residential and hotel destination on the Gold Coast, bringing together approximately 80 private residences, 60 hotel rooms and a collection of wellness, dining and leisure experiences.
What residence types are planned?+
The current concept includes one-, two- and three-bedroom residences, premium and sky residences, and a limited Crown Penthouse offering. The final mix, dimensions and pricing will be confirmed through design development.
When will residences be available to purchase?+
Sales timing will follow site confirmation, design development and the required approval pathway. Registering your interest places you on the list for verified project and release updates.
Are the images and floor plans final?+
No. The current imagery and brochure plans communicate the design intent only. Architecture, layouts, finishes, views, dimensions and amenities remain subject to the final site, consultant coordination and statutory approvals.

The company

Building the next expression of coastal living.

Tshoji Capital is a founder-led Australian property company focused on considered residential, hotel and mixed-use destinations. We begin with how a place should feel, then bring together the specialist partners required to make it real.

Our work is shaped by the Gold Coast: open horizons, a climate for life outdoors and an expectation that luxury should be effortless. Each project has its own identity, but every address is designed around arrival, private life, restoration and connection.

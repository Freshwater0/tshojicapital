# Tshoji Capital Page Comparison

## Executive Summary

The new page keeps the same core Tshoji Capital story, investment sections, contact details, and Orion image library. Its main improvement is the experience around that content: it moves from a quiet light editorial page to a more deliberate, premium investment presentation with a dark navy/brass default theme, richer hero imagery, clearer navigation feedback, more visual depth, and more interaction.

The new version is approximately **120 KB**, compared with approximately **91 KB** for the old version. The increase is primarily presentation logic, motion, theme support, and interaction rather than unnecessary content duplication.

## What Was Added

### 1. Dark/light theme switching

The new page introduces a dark navy and brass default theme plus a light theme alternative. A theme toggle in the header remembers the visitor's choice through `localStorage` and updates its accessible label and state.

**Why it is better:** The dark default gives the investment brand more authority and contrast, while the light option improves personal comfort and reading preference. Remembering the selection makes the experience feel complete rather than temporary.

### 2. Hero image carousel

The new hero includes nine Orion images, previous/next controls, numbered image dots, a caption, and accessible tab-style state updates. The hero now communicates the actual development immediately instead of relying mainly on decorative geometry.

**Why it is better:** Visitors see the product, not only the promise. The carousel creates a stronger first impression and gives the page a premium property-development character without forcing users to scroll before seeing visual evidence.

### 3. Scroll progress and return-to-top controls

The new page adds a scroll progress indicator and a visible top-of-page control.

**Why it is better:** This is a long-form page. These controls give visitors orientation and reduce the friction of moving through or exiting a lengthy investment narrative.

### 4. Active navigation feedback

Navigation links now respond to the section currently in view, with active styling and improved hover treatment.

**Why it is better:** Visitors can tell where they are in the page structure at a glance. This is especially useful when moving between plan, process, site, capital, founder, pipeline, FAQ, and contact content.

### 5. Stronger visual hierarchy

The new version adds:

- More structured hero metadata and caption treatment.
- Brass accent rules beneath section headings.
- More deliberate button styling and hover feedback.
- Deeper but restrained shadows around key visual surfaces.
- More refined uppercase navigation and control labels.
- Additional image hover treatment and card state changes.

**Why it is better:** The page now has clearer entry points and visual rhythm. Important content is easier to scan, while the investment tone feels more considered and less like a plain information document.

### 6. Richer motion system

The new version adds hero entrance animation, image drift, carousel transitions, scroll-triggered section states, card movement, light-sweep effects, and more responsive control feedback. It also includes reduced-motion handling for visitors who request less animation.

**Why it is better:** Motion gives the page a sense of quality and continuity. The reduced-motion rules keep that enhancement respectful and usable for accessibility-sensitive visitors.

### 7. More explicit contact action

The contact form now has a clearly styled submit action and improved responsive behavior alongside the existing name, email, and message fields.

**Why it is better:** The user journey ends with a more visible next step instead of leaving the form interaction visually unfinished.

## What Was Preserved

The new version keeps the important business content intact:

- The Tshoji Capital identity and Halcyon positioning.
- The plan, process, location, market, capital, founder, pipeline, FAQ, and contact sections.
- The Orion visual asset library in `assets/orion/`.
- Existing contact email and phone details.
- Responsive behavior and reduced-motion support.
- The long-form investor narrative rather than replacing it with a marketing-only landing page.

This is important because the redesign improves the delivery without discarding the information visitors need to evaluate the opportunity.

## Why the New Version Is Better

The new version is better for three practical reasons:

1. **It communicates value sooner.** The hero carousel presents Orion's spaces immediately, so the visitor understands the project visually before reading the full case.
2. **It makes a long page easier to navigate.** Active navigation, progress feedback, and a top shortcut reduce orientation cost across many sections.
3. **It feels more credible and intentional.** The navy/brass palette, restrained motion, typography, image treatment, and stronger section hierarchy create a more coherent investment presentation.

The result is a page that feels less like a static brochure and more like a considered digital investor experience, while retaining the old version's substantive content.


## Conclusion

The old page established the content foundation. The new page adds the product presentation layer: stronger art direction, clearer navigation, meaningful interaction, and a more premium brand tone. The improvement is not simply that it looks darker or more animated; it helps visitors understand the development, navigate the investment case, and reach the contact action with less friction.
